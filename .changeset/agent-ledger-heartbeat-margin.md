`@yolk-sdk/agent/tools`: the tool ledger keeps a renewal margin for one failed or hung heartbeat whatever the `heartbeatIntervalMs`, at any `leaseMs` of 4 ms or more. Each heartbeat is now bounded by `toolLedgerCompleteTimeoutMs` (5 s) or a sixth of `leaseMs`, whichever is shorter, and `heartbeatIntervalMs` is clamped to half the lease minus that bound (it was clamped to half the lease). Before, a lease of about 15 s or less, or an interval near half the lease, let one heartbeat that failed or timed out lapse the lease of a running call: a concurrent duplicate then reported the call as abandoned ("may already have been applied") while it ran and recorded its success. The margin covers one late heartbeat when the claim and the other heartbeats are fast; it is exactly zero at the defaults (30 s lease, 10 s interval, 5 s heartbeat timeout, unchanged), and several slow heartbeats or a stalled process can still let the lease lapse. On short leases, heartbeat (and claim) latency must now stay under `min(5 s, leaseMs / 6)`: a store whose heartbeats took between a sixth of the lease and 5 s used to renew and now times out every heartbeat (for example a 6 s lease with 1.2 s heartbeats), so give a slow store a longer lease.
