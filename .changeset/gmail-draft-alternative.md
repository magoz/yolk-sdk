Fix Gmail drafts getting hard-wrapped at about 70 columns when a person opens them in Gmail web and clicks Send. `gmail.draft_compose`, `gmail.draft_update`, and `gmail.draft_reply` used to write one unencoded `text/plain` part, which Gmail web opens in its plain-text composer and wraps on send. A plain-text body is now sent as 7-bit `multipart/alternative`: a text/plain part with the exact body and a text/html part derived from it (HTML-escaped, `<br>` per line break, runs of spaces kept, `http(s)` URLs linked to themselves, inside one `<div dir="ltr">`), both `charset=UTF-8` and quoted-printable with lines of at most 76 characters, under the constant boundary `=_yolk-draft-alternative` (which quoted-printable output cannot contain); the headers gain `MIME-Version: 1.0`. The three actions also take an optional `contentType: 'text' | 'html'` (`text` when omitted, as on the Outlook draft actions; new export `GmailDraftContentType`); `html` sends the body as one quoted-printable text/html part. `gmail.get_thread` no longer applies a part's `Content-Transfer-Encoding` to `body.data` again: Gmail answers it already transfer-decoded, so quoted-printable text holding `=XX` (such as `?date=2026`) was corrupted and base64-encoded parts lost their body. `gmail.send_message` is unchanged. The synthetic draft conformance fixture and `@yolk-sdk/emulators/google` follow the new draft MIME (the draft payload is `multipart/alternative`, its metadata `body.size` 0).
