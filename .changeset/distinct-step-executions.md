Retried and redelivered model steps no longer splice or duplicate answers in the client, for hosts that tag their model events and clients on this release (#305).

- `@yolk-sdk/vercel-workflows`: `sequenceDurableAgentEvent`, `writeDurableAgentEvent` and `durableAgentEventId` take an optional `executionId`. It is appended to the id (`${streamId}:${turn}:${eventSequence}:${executionId}`) and set as the event's `executionId` field. Every execution of a step starts from the same `eventSequence`, so before, a retried model step (`modelStepRetry`, platform `fn.maxRetries`, queue redelivery) wrote a different answer under the first execution's ids and `eventId` de-dupe showed parts of both. Hosts must pass one unique per execution (for example `${stepId}:${attempt}` from `getStepMetadata()`) on every event the model call produces, `LLMStreamStart` included, and leave it off host events (promoted user messages, the terminal event written after the commit); a receipt replay rewrites only that terminal event. Without it ids are unchanged. Tool-batch steps are out of scope.
- `@yolk-sdk/agent`: events have an optional `executionId`. `reduceAgentEvents`, `applyAgentEventToChatProjection` and the `useAgentChat` reducer keep a per-turn execution ledger (`executions` on their state; carry it with `seenEventIds`): a newer execution's `LLMStreamStart` removes the older one's provisional output (streaming text, its `AssistantMessage`, tool calls nothing acknowledged) and drops its later events; a later turn, terminal event, user message or `AgentStart` settles executions and drops their later events; at `AgentEnd`, a final turn that ran more than once shows the committed answer. Events without `executionId` only discard output still streaming at the next `LLMStreamStart`. Upgrade clients and servers together: an older client ignores the field and shows both executions back to back. Still open: a non-final turn whose older execution committed while a newer one streamed shows the newer text until reload.
