Expand `@yolk-sdk/connectors/fortnox` from 14 to 91 actions (42 `read`, 31 `write`, 18 `destructive`) covering nine more Fortnox scopes, each with an opt-in slot kept off `FortnoxCombinedOAuthCredentialSlot`: articles (`article`), price lists and prices (`price`), cost centers (`costcenter`), invoice and supplier-invoice payments including bookkeeping (`payment`), offers including convert to order/invoice, cancel, email, and mark sent (`offer`), print templates (`print`), inbox folders and deletion (`inbox`), company settings, locked period, predefined accounts, labels, modes of payment, terms of payment and delivery, ways of delivery, and units (`settings`), and the token's user profile (`profile`). Deletes, payment bookkeeping, and offer lifecycle actions are `destructive` because the Fortnox API cannot undo them; new write inputs reject unknown keys. New host-only helpers: `downloadFortnoxOfferPreview`, `downloadFortnoxInboxFile`, and `uploadFortnoxInboxFile` (one multipart POST through `ConnectorBinaryWriteHttpClient`, capped at `fortnoxInboxUploadMaxBytes`). Customer-invoice send/book stay absent; Company Logo and the sales API are not modeled (no documented endpoints). Endpoints follow Fortnox's API reference and archived resource pages and are not yet observed live; the Fortnox emulator serves their reads with unverified synthetic shapes and none of their writes.
